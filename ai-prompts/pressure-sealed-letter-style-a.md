## Customer prompt (copy everything in the box)

```text
(Paste this into Grok, ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI design tool.)
You are helping a Yellow Letter HQ customer design the Pressure Sealed Letter, style A. Follow these steps in order.

STEP 1 – START FROM THIS BLANK TEMPLATE (do not change it)
- Trim size: 8.5 x 11 in
- Bleed: none
- Safe margin: 0.25 in inside the trim on every side
- Locked zones – YLHQ prints these. Nothing may be placed in them; keep 0.125 in extra clearance around each:
- sheet, page 1: Recipient address – x 1.61–4.30 in, y 9.80–10.52 in
- sheet, page 1: YLHQ production marks – x 6.72–8.02 in, y 8.36–8.55 in
- sheet, page 2: USPS barcode (IMb) – x 0.33–4.11 in, y 3.64–4.13 in
- sheet, page 2: Return address – x 3.05–7.90 in, y 6.68–7.30 in
- sheet, page 2: Postage indicia – x 0.78–1.58 in, y 6.57–7.08 in
- sheet, page 2: YLHQ production marks – x 6.85–7.77 in, y 6.56–6.77 in
- sheet, both pages: Pressure-seal fold / glue panels – confirmed at proof
- YLHQ locks ONLY the USPS and print requirements: trim, bleed, safe margin, folds, window, recipient address, USPS barcode, postage/indicia, return address and small production marks. Everything else is the customer's choice – layout, colors, images, copy and where each personal detail goes. Do not add design rules of your own.

STEP 2 – INTERVIEW THE CUSTOMER BEFORE DESIGNING
Ask these questions ONE AT A TIME and wait for each answer. For each placement question, offer 2–3 spots inside the safe area and outside the locked zones:
1. How should we greet the recipient? Options: "Hi {{FirstName}}," – "Dear {{FullName}}," – "Dear {{LastName}} Family," – or no name.
2. Do you want the property address in the copy (e.g. "I'd like to make an offer on {{PropertyAddress}}")? Optionally with {{PropertyCity}}.
3. Where should your phone number ({{AgentPhone}}) go? Options: a large call-to-action line under the message – in your signature block – a banner across the top – a P.S. line.
4. Where should your name ({{AgentName}}) go? Options: signature under the message – header at the top – next to your photo or logo.
5. Do you want a variable offer amount ({{Offer}})? If yes, where: in the headline – inside a message sentence – in a callout box. Also show it written out ({{OfferInWords}})?
6. Do you want the mailing date ({{MailingDate}}) printed on the piece? If yes, where?
7. What should the outside panel say, and what goes on the inside message panel?
Only use these placeholders, typed exactly: {{AgentName}}, {{AgentPhone}}, {{FullName}}, {{Offer}}, {{MailingDate}}, {{OfferInWords}}, {{FirstName}}, {{LastName}}, {{PropertyAddress}}, {{PropertyCity}}. Never invent placeholders, and never draw addresses, barcodes, stamps or postage.

STEP 3 – FONTS
Printed text uses ONLY these YLHQ fonts: Arial, Arial Black, Bookman Old Style, Calibri, Courier New, Myriad Pro, PremiumUltra16, PremiumUltra26, Times New Roman. If the customer asks for another font, offer the closest one from this list.

STEP 4 – GENERATE THE DESIGN
- Build the design on the blank template, putting each placeholder exactly where the customer chose.
- Images: 300 ppi or more at final size (lower prints only if the customer accepts it at upload; under 150 ppi will not print). Use only images and logos the customer owns or has rights to.
- Output: a print-ready PDF at trim + bleed, one page per side, fonts embedded, placeholders left as live text. If you can only make images, give one PNG or JPG per side at that size and at least 300 ppi.
- Finish with a short list of where each placeholder was placed.
```
