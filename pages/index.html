New (Nov 17): Letter templates and envelopes in even grids

  • #/letters/no10 and every other letter product, including handwritten cards · Templates and envelopes sit side by side at the top, each in a 3-across grid of the same tile size, with a divider down the middle. On a phone they stack
  • Postage, letter font, envelope font, and the other options (lead type, extras, mail schedule) are small dropdowns under the grids. The template still sets the default font, and every font from before is available if you want to change it
  • A compact Upload your list & artwork bar opens the field mapper when you add a list. Quantity is filled from the cleaned count. Pricing stays behind See pricing. Notes include a small Select files control for any other information

New (Nov 17): Letters layout, merged check / windowed

  • #/letters · Product tiles are a little larger. “Letter with Windowed Envelope” and “Check Letter” are now one product: Check Letter (Windowed Envelope). The old #/letters/windowed link redirects there
  • #/letters/no10 and every other letter product, including handwritten cards · Templates are on the left and the envelope pane is on the right, at the top of the form. Then come the list upload, a short What’s this? for the upload tool, lead type and artwork, then notes, extras and the mail schedule
  • The price table is behind a See pricing button. The form itself shows Standard or First Class postage, quantity, and Add to cart
  • A template sets the letter font, ink and paper, and those stay locked. A Change envelope font button opens a dropdown if you want a different envelope font. You can still add a second page and switch envelope types

New (Nov 17): FAQ answers, PDF receipt, Ana feedback on FAQ and the order form

  • #/faq and the standalone FAQ · Updated with Estefania Martinez’s confirmed answers and Josh’s Nov 17 decisions: up to 12 batches, no scanner fee, a family photo does not cost extra, pressure-sealed letters take about 3 business days, lists are CSV only, no tracking phone numbers, if a list is under the minimum you pay the minimum and we mail fewer pieces, and payment is credit card or ACH only (split payments across credit cards; payment reminders stay on). Invoice Me is unchanged
  • The letter and card order form batch menu now goes up to 12 batches (it stopped at 8)
  • Custom envelope design is 3 cents per piece, plus the $65 design fee, on every product that offers it
  • List upload on order pages and in the Budget Maximizer tool now says CSV only (no Excel XLS or XLSX), matching the FAQ. The file picker is limited to .csv, and a non-CSV file is treated as artwork or instructions, not as a list
  • #/detail/204518 and #/orders · You can download a receipt in PDF format from Order Details and from the orders list. The button is labeled Download receipt (PDF) on the details page and Receipt (PDF) in the list. It saves a file named YLHQ-Receipt-plus-the-order-number, with the company name, order number, date, billing name, items, quantity, price, postage, totals, masked payment method, and paid status
  • #/faq and the standalone FAQ · Ana Alastre’s Nov 17 feedback: the Budget Maximizer is the list-upload process on every order (not a separate tool), and you pay at checkout only for records that will actually be mailed after undeliverable addresses and duplicates are removed; “What is Text by Mail?” is rewritten in plain steps; step-by-step answers use numbered lists or bullets; and the $19 undeliverable list is explained as the addresses the address check flagged and removed before mailing, which customers can add as an extra option when they order
  • #/letters, #/postcards, and #/rapid · The order form is grouped into Design, Optional extras, Mail schedule, Notes, and Your list, with shorter labels and “Learn more” help for first-time customers. Field mapping is one table (our field next to your column) with problems flagged, and optional fields sit behind a toggle. Both Add to cart buttons, postage tabs, and Nonprofit-hidden-unless-approved are unchanged

New (Nov 15–16): saved template record, same minimums, Thursday drop day

  • #/custom-template/approve · Approving a new mail piece saves a template record (TPL-1002 and up) with the product, size, paper, ink, envelope, postage, return address and phone source, and design files
  • #/rapid · Ready to mail card is built from that saved template record, not a past order, with a small “Source: saved template” line. Same minimum as a regular order of that product (200 pieces for letters, 100 cards First Class or 200 Standard for postcards). Thursday is an allowed drop day (order by Saturday 3:30 PM PT); Tuesday stays off
  • #/rapid-proofs · Proof card shows the saved template source, the product’s normal minimum (“Qty (min 200)”) and normal tier pricing; below the minimum is blocked. Drop day menu includes Thursday
  • #/cart · The mailing line comes only from the saved template; the $65 template fee stays its own line and is never copied into the mailing order

New (Nov 15): template freeze fix + Rapid custom template

  • #/postcards/fc425 · Freeze fixed: tap a template to pick it (instant check mark, then it scrolls to the next step). Small Enlarge button opens the big viewer. Sticky step bar with live total
  • #/letters/no10 · Same template fix on every letter form, plus the sticky step bar and toasts
  • #/letters/greeting · Greeting card templates: tap picks, Enlarge zooms
  • #/custom-template · Design a New Mail Piece for Rapid: saved default return address and phone (fill personal fields, list column overrides), no How many templates, $65 + $65 custom envelope line, Cursive/Print for handwritten envelopes, personal field chips, Review tick fixed
  • #/custom-template/approve · New: template proof approval screen. Approve puts the template in Rapid Orders as Ready to mail
  • #/rapid · Ready to mail card: built from this saved template (now uses the same minimums as a regular order, see Nov 16). Thursday drop day added

New (Nov 15): postage tabs, nonprofit, start dates

Postage dropdown removed (tabs only). Nonprofit postage is hidden unless the account is nonprofit-authorized; its price is Standard − $0.435 + $0.225 (USPS 2026 nonprofit Marketing Mail letter rate). Campaign start dates follow the live site's cutoff rules.

  • #/letters/no10 · #10 letter form: the old Postage dropdown is gone. Postage is picked only with the tabs in the price box. New campaign start date calendar (Mon/Wed/Fri only, past-cutoff dates greyed out), defaulting to the earliest open date, with the cutoff rule and "Minimum 200 pieces" shown
  • #/postcards/fc425 · 4.25 x 5.5 postcard: postage tabs only (no dropdown), start date calendar, "Minimum 100 cards First Class, 200 cards Standard"
  • #/postcards/half · Half Page postcard: same changes
  • #/postcards/hw · Real handwritten postcard: start date uses the Actual Handwritten lead time (5 days of production after proof approval)
  • #/letters/hwcardsnotes · Real handwritten cards and notes: Actual Handwritten lead time on the start date
  • #/account · My Account: new "Nonprofit postage authorized (demo)" switch. Turn it on to see the Nonprofit tab and price row on letter forms
  • #/rapid · Rapid Orders step 3: same calendar, using the Rapid Order production-day rule

Design polish (Nov 15)

One button system for the whole mockup: yellow primary, white secondary and dark buttons, all rounded pills with the same heights and type. Brand yellow #FFC523 and its hover #EDA700 (from yellowletterhq.com) everywhere. No prices, products or features changed.

  • #/postcards · All Templates is now a yellow pill button, the same family as every other primary button. The 5 postcards sit 3 + 2, centred
  • #/postcards/fc425 · Order form: one "selected" look for postage, options and fonts, and a new Estimated total + Add to cart bar at the end of the form (after the list upload)
  • #/letters · Letter picker: 3 even columns (3 x 3), names no longer break onto 4-5 lines
  • #/letters/no10 · #10 letter form: pill buttons, matching option pills, the same end-of-form Add to cart bar
  • #/letters/greeting · Greeting cards: yellow pill See All Templates button, the same end-of-form bar
  • #/custom-template · Design a New Mail Piece: 3 x 3 even grid, every Choose this button lined up, steps centred
  • #/home · Home: By the Numbers cards are equal width, Shop Now buttons match the rest
  • #/shop · Shop: one button size and shape on every card
  • #/orders · My Orders: action buttons in the same pill style, page title on the shared title size
  • #/proof/204518 · Proof page: Approve / Disapprove use the same pill shape
