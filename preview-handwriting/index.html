Step 1

Print your sheet

Two pages: page 1 has letters, page 2 has numbers and punctuation. Print on Letter paper at 100% (“Actual size”).

  • Turn off “Fit to page”
  • Page 1: 52 letter boxes (A–Z, a–z)

Step 2

Write in blue or black pen

One character per box. Sit each letter on the dashed line and don’t touch the box edges.

  • Use a blue or dark black ballpoint or gel pen
  • No pencil, no light colors
  • Takes about ten minutes
